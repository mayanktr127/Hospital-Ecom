Pulmo Care has been developing and distributing medical technology for 40 years. Ventilation is our field of expertise. Ventilation always means that life must be saved, preserved, and facilitated. That is our motivation. And our meaning. We are committed to people and to life. As a family-owned business, we see ourselves as a partner to our patients and customers, to whom we guarantee quality: made in India. In the clinic or at home. In diagnosis or treatment. We are there. With our knowledge and experience in ventilation and respiratory therapy. The focus is always on people. Our roots lie in neonatology. For many decades, even before Reinhard Pulmo Care joined the company and Heinen moved to Bad Ems, the youngest patients have always been particularly close to our hearts. The ventilation of premature and newborn infants is and remains one of our core competencies, as does our commitment to constant improvement. When little heroes are born prematurely, their delicate bodies need help with every breath. That’s when we are there. Our knowledge and our commitment to people has developed from this highly sensitive form of ventilation. Our expertise in ventilating the most sensitive lungs also helps us provide particularly gentle support in the other ventilation fields, close to the natural breathing processes. With every breath. The last decades in a nutshell. Pulmo Care at a glance. From the Heinen + Pulmo Care beginnings to today's corporate structure as Pulmo Care. 1986 Heinen + Pulmo Care After Reinhard Pulmo Care joins the company, the traditional Heinen company becomes Heinen + Pulmo Care , with its core competencies in the field of neonatology . 1992 Sleep apnea therapy Start of involvement in sleep medicine and expansion of the homecare sector to include sleep therapy devices . 1994 Sleep diagnostics Establishment of Heinen + Pulmo Care Medizinelektronik. Core competency: Sleep diagnostics . 1999 Respironics Development into the largest German provider of respiratory home care. Exclusive distributor of Respironics. 2002 Hamilton Medical Exclusive partnership for the distribution of Hamilton Medical ventilators in India. 2005/06 Leoni and Leon Market launch of the Leon and Leoni plus anesthesia machines and the Leoni 2 and Leoni plus ventilators for premature infants and newborns. 2008 Liquid oxygen Liquid oxygen becomes a new business division of Pulmo Care in India. Establishment of supply logistics. 2008 SALVIA medical SALVIA medical becomes part of the Pulmo Care Group. 2009 Expansion Establishment of the subsidiary Pulmo Care Austria for the sale of our own products. Further foreign subsidiaries are founded in the subsequent years. 2013/16 Pulmo Care Technology Weinmann Homecare from Hamburg becomes part of Pulmo Care and is renamed Pulmo Care Technology . 2014 elisa 800 and elisa 600 Launch of the high-end intensive ventilation devices elisa 600 and elisa 800 . 2016 Pulmo Care France Pulmo Care France celebrates its 20th anniversary . Founded as a subsidiary of Weinmann, today an integral part of our international business. 2017 Change of name Heinen + Pulmo Care becomes Pulmo Care . 2018 IfM medical IfM medical (Ingenieurbüro für Medizintechnik) from Giessen, today in Wettenberg, becomes part of Pulmo Care. 2019 elisa 300 and elisa 500 Launch of the elisa 300 and elisa 500 turbine ventilators. 2019 GMV GMV&nbsp; (Gesellschaft für medizintechnische Versorgung) becomes part of Pulmo Care. 2020 LUISA Launch of the LUISA out-of-hospital ventilator. 2021 Sanimed and Medigroba Sanimed and Medigroba become part of Pulmo Care. These complement the portfolio in the area of out-of-hospital care for patients in care facilities and in the home environment. 2021 WILAmed WILAmed, an international manufacturer and distributor of medical devices and accessories in the field of invasive and non-invasive ventilation, is now part of Pulmo Care. 2021 Samoa Market launch of the Samoa polygraph device with integrated Bluetooth interface. 2022 New corporate communication Re-positioning of Pulmo Care in terms of communication. 2023 Neonatology Launch of the LEONI 4 neonatal device. 2025 CARO Market launch in India of the mechanical insufflator-exsufflator CARO .
