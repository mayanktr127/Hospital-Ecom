The Pulmo Care Academy has long been a fixed part of our service philosophy. With our therapy programs, courses and information materials, we are happy to do our part to support you and share our knowledge with you. In the six subject areas of anesthesia, ventilation, diagnostics, intensive care ventilation, neonatology and sleep apnea therapy, our customers and anyone else who is interested can find out about the latest findings, methods and research. You have the opportunity to find out about our learning opportunities, register for face-to-face trainings or take part in self-study online courses and web-based trainings via the platform. In addition, our knowledge base serves as a library for reference and reading. Here you will find manuals, instructions for use, scripts, videos, tutorials &nbsp;and many other resources.
